## Chapter 1: Who you know
### Scene 1.1: Surely they're not dead...
[[JJ Sin]] collects [[The Next of Kin]] to tell them that their loved ones haven't returned from the latest run, and give them any 'life insurance' payouts. The party need to decide not to accept this outcome, and to look into what actually happened. 
JJ doesn't know much, because the Johnson didn't include him in the details. He won't tell them who the Johnson was, and says they need to make a name for themselves before the Johnson will even consider meeting them anyway. 
His only other lead is that the Crew asked for him to set them up with an information broker, Splinter, during the run. But if they want his contact information, they better prove that they can handle themselves first.
### Scene 1.2: You gotta start somewhere
After giving the team time to get any implants and spend their resources, JJ calls them to meet [[Auntie Mira]] for their first run.
The temple was expecting a delivery, which was waylaid by local gangers. The runners are asked to get it back.
