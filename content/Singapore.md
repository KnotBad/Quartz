Singapore is split into 5 main Regions.
- The [[Central Region]], home to the main CBD and hub of Singapore.
- The [[East Region]], home to Changi Airport and the Tampines and Bedok residential zones, where many upper-middle class people live.
- The [[North Region]], the greenest region in Singapore. Many richer folk call this region home.
- The [[North-East Region]], a mostly residential region full of public housing. Includes the [[Hougang Containment Zone (HCZ)]] area.
- The [[West Region]], which is largely industrial (especially as you get further southwest). Includes [[Float Town]].
