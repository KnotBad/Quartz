# Float Town
## Summary
**Type:** Neighbourhood
**Located:** [[West Region]], around the remains of Jurong Island
**Controlling Faction(s):** N/A
**Notable NPCs:** 
**Security Level:** Z; High Threat Response time is usually 2d6 hours
**Notes:** One of the two main Barrens of Singapore. Built upon the ashes of the Jurong Island Industrial Estate, Float Town isn't even acknowledged as legitimate by the Singaporean board. A mish-mash of stilt houses and houseboats, there is no permanent bridge to Float Town.

## Details
**History:** Jurong Island, off the south west coast of Singapore, was once the largest island in a small archipelago including Pulau Ayer Chawan, Pulau Ayer Merbau, Pulau Merlimau and Pulau Seraya. In the late 20th century, as the petrochemical industry expanded in Singapore and the city-state ran out of industrial land, the Government of Singapore (under the coordination of the [[JTC Corporation]]) began land reclamation efforts, turning the archipelago into one larger industrial island, called Jurong Island. Construction finished in 2009, and many petrochemical companies moved in.

Over the rest of the 21st Century, [[Mitsuhama Computer Technologies|Mitsuhama]] either built or bought most of the factories and plants on the island. They had a very flexible deal with the Singaporean government, allowing them to treat the workers incredibly poorly. Over time, this led to the Jurong Island Strike of 2059. Neither side was able to reach an agreement, until eventually on December 12th 2059 the Island was bombed, killing hundreds of protesting workers, destroying much of the industry and undoing the work of the land reclamation efforts. MCT has denied any involvement in the bombing, though the workers union are adamant that it was the corporation. 

**Modern Day:** The ruins of Jurong Island are now home to thousands of the poor and SINless residents of Singapore. Stilt houses built from the ruins of the factories stretch across and between the islands, with house boats and haphazard walkways weaving between. The residents refer to the area as "Float Town," though the Board of Singapore Incorporated don't recognise it as any more than a ruin.
Floaters (as the residents call themselves) have a diet heavy in fish. As the land and water are heavily polluted by the local industry and the huge amounts of petrochemicals that spilled during the attack, this means that locals are often sick.
The community is a young one. Many of the people involved in the worker strikes remain, with some continuing to fight corporate interests in Singapore. There is a faction trying to legitimise Float Town, which is opposed by the criminals, SINless and gangers who have begun to call Float Town home.
