## Shadowrun Summary
It's 2078 and the world has changed. Corporations are more powerful than nations, with the biggest corps - the AAAs - calling all the shots. Extraterritoriality means that on corporate owned land, the corporation is the law and is not subject to regulation by the nation it happens to be within. Magic has returned, and with it all manner of weirdness - metahumanity includes elves, dwarves, orks and trolls (among others), dragons and spirits walk the Earth, and people have learnt how manipulate magic - either by traditional spellcasting or by channelling it physically, as an adept. The digital landscape has also evolved, with Augmented Reality being the norm and Virtual Reality being easily accessed through Direct Neural Interfaces (DNI). Advances in human augmentation also mean that it's common to see people with more chrome than flesh.
Shadowrunners are basically criminals for hire. You work for whoever will pay, and often end up being a layer of plausible deniability for the corps' dirty work. Remember; shoot straight, conserve ammo, and never make a deal with a dragon!
## Campaign Brief
You live in [[Singapore]], Incorporated. You aren't a Shadowrunner (yet!), but someone close to you *is* - and whether you're aware of it or not, they chose you to be their **Next of Kin**. If they don't come home, their Fixer has your number and instructions to ring.
Sure enough, you're about to get that call. Your loved one is in trouble - and you and your new friends are the only people who care enough to try to do something about it.

We'll play a Prelude session as pre-generated Prime Runners as we learn the system. Feel free to give me notes for your Prime Runner, but don't stress about building them. This run will drop straight into the action, and *you will fail it.* 
Then, I will help everyone build their main characters and we will begin the actual campaign, to find out what happened in the Prelude.
## Mechanical Notes
### General
We are using Shadowrun 5e. I will share a folder with all the sourcebooks, but you don't need to know all of them! I will have a list of which  books are relevant to which character archetype. Players will be expected to understand the general mechanics of their characters.
### Singapore Rules
#### Hard on Drugs
Singapore is particularly hard on drugs. All Drugs have availability increased by 2, cost increased by 50 Y, and are Forbidden. 
Exceptions are listed here:
- Jazz remains Restricted, but only for law enforcement personnel - a fake SIN with a license for Jazz must also list the occupation as law enforcement.
- Long Haul remains unrestricted.
### Character Creation Rules
Characters will be made using Standard Character Creation Rules (starting CRB, p62) or Sum to Ten Rules (from Run Faster, p62). 
#### Free Stuff
All characters will be given:
- A Rating 1 Commlink
	- Meta Link (CRB p 439) 
	- If desired, this can be a different brand to better fit your backstory
- A set of Trodes
	- CRB p439
	- If you have a datajack, you don't also need to take the trodes.
- A Rating 1 Fake SIN with no additional licenses
	- Gear listing on CRB p443, more details on CRB p 367
	- Rating 1: "Random anybody, age, nationality, and sex may not match; no supporting data"
- A Fixer Contact
	- JJ Sins: Loyalty 1, Connection 6
#### Character Options
Banned options are out. Restricted options will only be allowed with good reason, and only at GM discretion.
##### Metatypes
###### Banned
- Metasapients, including: Centaurs, Nagas, Sasquatches and Pixies (blame Prithvi)
- HMHVV Infected Characters
- Drakes
- AIs
###### Restricted
- Changelings
- Shifters
##### Qualities
Note: Negative qualities need to be impactful. Don't take negatives to actions you are never going to take.
###### Banned
**Positive Qualities**:
- **Common Sense** - I don't like the idea of a mechanical impetus for the GM to tell a player "Hey, your idea is dumb." Also, I know myself and will forget to do this.
- **Human Lifespan** - We aren't risking dying to old age. Don't spend 10 Karma on this, we can just have that be in backstory.
- **Latent Dracomorphosis** - Being a Drake is still banned
**Negative Qualities:**
- **Leeeeeeeeroy Jenkins** - Fun joke. Either never triggers, or is just a bad time for the table.
- **Superhuman Psychosis** - Often, the combat bonus means this is more positive than negative. 
###### Restricted
- ALL Qualities from **Forbidden Arcana** - This book was whack, talk to me before taking anything from it.
**Positive Qualities:**
- **Friends in High Places** - Will need to discuss the contacts created
**Negative Qualities:**
- **Borrowed Time** - Will need real good backstory here.
- **Code of Honour** - Run it by me first, and I'll be wanting to make sure it's not going to impact the team too harshly
- **Earther** - We're probably not going into space.
- **Incompetent** - Discuss first, will not allow if it's not something your character would be asked to do.
###### Changes
**Negative Qualities:**
- **Allergy** - Add text "If a character is able to fully mitigate their allergy (for example, with bioware), then they must buy off this quality at the normal cost of double the Karma gained. Any Y spent on this mitigation can be considered as part of this cost, at the usual 1 K <-> 2000 Y rate."
