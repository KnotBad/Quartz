Hougang houses the [[Buangkok Green Medical Park]], which itself contained the [[Singapore Leprosy Relief Association (SILRA)]]. At the onset of HMHVV, SILRA was a natural place for the infected in Singapore to be treated. This has led to Hougang having a much higher than average ghoul population, and being barricaded off from the rest of the city. For decades now, the HCZ has been where Singapore's ghouls are taken.

The authorities don't much care who enters the HCZ, but they do keep a close eye on those leaving. Official checkpoints are dotted around the perimeter and operate from 10 AM to 4 PM, where city officials scan the SIN of everyone attempting to leave. You must have evidence of being tested for HMHVV within the last month on your SIN to be let out.
Of course, most residents know how to leave the HCZ without being spotted. A number of businesses dot the border which allow entry from either side of the barricade, and there are plenty of ways to climb over the fence - just make sure the patrolling drones aren't looking.
